How It Works

Three Steps to One PDF

From upload to download in under a minute.

1

Upload Your PDFs

Open the app and use the file picker or drag-and-drop area to upload two or more PDF files you want to combine.

2

Merge with One Click

Click the Merge PDFs button. The app processes your files instantly using the pypdf library — no waiting around.

3

Download Your File

A download button appears with your combined PDF ready to save. Done — it's that simple.
